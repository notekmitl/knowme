# Noto Sans Thai

Bundled files:

- `NotoSansThai-Regular.ttf`
- `NotoSansThai-Bold.ttf`
- `NotoSans-Regular.ttf`
- `NotoSans-Bold.ttf`

Source: the official archived Noto Fonts repository:
<https://github.com/notofonts/noto-fonts/tree/main/hinted/ttf/NotoSansThai>
and <https://github.com/notofonts/noto-fonts/tree/main/hinted/ttf/NotoSans>.

License source: the maintained Noto Thai repository:
<https://github.com/notofonts/thai/blob/main/OFL.txt>

The font files are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 included in
`OFL.txt`. Only the Regular and Bold weights used by the PDF exporter are
bundled. Noto Sans supplies deterministic Latin, digit, and punctuation glyphs
that are not present in the static Noto Sans Thai files.
