# PDF geometry and visual QA

Five final PDFs render to 30 pages total (6 each). Every page was rasterized with Poppler at 110 DPI and visually inspected. Result: no blank/footer-only page, clipping, overlap, text outside printable bounds, broken Thai wrapping, incorrect footer number or appendix-before-ending defect. Each applicable future horizon visibly contains work, money, love and health; the synthesis precedes methodology.
