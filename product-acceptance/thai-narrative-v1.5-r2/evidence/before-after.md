# Before / after narrative comparison

R1 measured 55 sentences / 267 measured words versus its 107-sentence / 392-word baseline, largely because eight of twelve future domain blocks were removed. R2 restores 12/12 domain-horizon blocks for Known and Unknown fixtures and retains the established 6-page Known / 5-page Unknown pagination baseline; depth is restored rather than replaced with filler.

Before: a horizon headline could highlight love while the body contained only money, or highlight work while the body contained only health. After: every horizon renders work, money, love and health, so every highlighted domain is present in that horizon body.

Before: biography-like statements asserted comparisons at school, family influence, network opportunity, or silent expectations as events. After: each is explicitly a theme/pattern to compare with lived experience.

Before: the final narrative advice was generic. After: the pre-appendix synthesis prioritises quality of work, financial/decision space, and relationship boundaries/recovery.
