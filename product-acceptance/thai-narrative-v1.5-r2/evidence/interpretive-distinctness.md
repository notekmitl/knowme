# Interpretive cross-profile distinctness

Scope excludes headings, birth-data lines, methodology, disclosures, limitations, page numbers and static safety text. It evaluates Claim Ledger primary interpretive expressions only.

Final sample: 84 interpretive claims across five fixtures. Exact cross-fixture prose groups are accepted only where the complete consumer-material evidence signature is identical. Result: 22 exact cross-fixture groups, 22 materially identical signatures, 0 different-signature violations.

The evidence signature records horizon, domain, band, consumer risk authority, evidence availability, transition state, evidence key, ownership and time dependency. Raw `pressure` is normalized to its domain-specific consumer risk because that typed projection is the actual authority consumed by prose.
