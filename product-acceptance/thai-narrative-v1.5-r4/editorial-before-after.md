# Editorial before/after — R3 → R4

## Opening

R3 Known and Unknown both opened with `ลายเซ็นของคำอ่าน: งานเดินหน้าได้ เมื่อเงินพร้อมใช้ยังไม่ถูกเบียด` and the same body, including the unnatural phrase `กดให้เงินพร้อมใช้ยังไม่ถูกเบียดยากขึ้น`.

R4 Owner Known opens with `งานจะไปต่อได้ เมื่อข้อตกลงยังชัดและทำได้จริง`, ties endurance to the current life period, introduces relationship capacity as the consequence, and ends with a decision question. R4 Unknown opens separately with `ให้สิ่งที่เกิดซ้ำจริงนำทาง ก่อนขยับงาน`, uses observable recurrence rather than time-dependent evidence, and asks whether growth still leaves room for other parts of life.

## Past

R3 repeated `นี่เป็นกรอบตั้งคำถาม ไม่ใช่ข้อสรุป...` for each past period. R4 states the evidence boundary once, then gives a period-specific `ธีมสำหรับทบทวน` and `คำถามสะท้อน` containing that period's name, age band and theme.

## Current

R3 exposed forecast claims through the ledger but did not render 20 current primary expressions. R4 renders `สิ่งที่ต้องตัดสินใจตอนนี้` as the first canonical forecast window and records its exact rendered domain bodies in the ledger.

## Twelve months

R3 used repeated long-range suffix families. R4 uses observable triggers: scope changes, usable income, behavior that repeats, and recovery after heavy weeks. Each trigger states how it changes the decision.

## Next life period

R3 often repeated the same advice with a horizon label. R4 describes direction and consequence: what changes after the current period, what is retained or handed off, and which supporting domain constrains the move.

## Unknown time

R3 appended `และต้องยืนยันจากสิ่งที่เกิดซ้ำเพราะไม่มีเวลาเกิด` across domain paragraphs. R4 places one uncertainty disclosure before forecast and detailed limitations in methodology; domain prose itself contains no repeated no-time suffix and makes no Lagna/house claim.
