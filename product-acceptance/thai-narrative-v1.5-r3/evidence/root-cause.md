# R3 narrative root cause and correction

R2 still composed twelve forecast blocks independently from a fixed horizon/domain sentence table. It appended internal priority/transition labels to consumer prose and treated matching evidence signatures as a reason to exempt exact reuse. That proved correctness but did not create a report-specific reading flow.

R3 introduces a report-level narrative plan derived from forecast band, risk, transition, top supported theme and current life period. It selects at most two motifs and supplies one central tension, consequence and decision question before any forecast block is rendered. Horizon copy now has distinct authority: current pressure/decision, 12-month trigger, and next-period direction/consequence.

The past path was also corrected: source assertions that could read like invented biography are no longer emitted. Each past period is a cautious synthesis plus a question that explicitly asks the reader to compare against real memory. Unknown-time continues to omit Lagna/house/time-dependent astrology.

Consumer copy no longer contains `น้ำหนักเด่น`, `น้ำหนักปานกลาง`, `น้ำหนักเบา` or `คาบเกี่ยวรอยต่อ`. Exact strong-claim freshness is measured directly on rendered consumer bodies, without a domain-signature blanket exemption.
