   The report avoids list-like score labels and one-line system status copy.
3. **Does the copy defend itself or hedge? — No.** Reader predictions contain no

   end rather than interrupting each prediction.

   work, money, relationship, load, recovery and support movements. Imperative
   actions are isolated in `คำแนะนำสั้น ๆ` after the summary.
5. **Is there personality or psychology copy? — No.** The report does not infer
   personality, hidden motives, trauma, self-worth or an inner identity.
6. **Does past copy state changes directly? — Yes.** Each past age period names
   a change in household conditions, learning/work/social setting, responsibility,
   work role or agreement. It asks no reflective question and does not tell the
   reader to remember an event.
7. **Where does meaning repeat? — No unintended duplicate was found.** Work,
   finance, relationship, health and support sections own different changes.
   The 12-month section limits those changes to the exact rolling range. The
   final summary intentionally compresses the arc and introduces no new claim;
   it is not an additional mapped prediction.
8. **Which sentence reads like a template or system message? — None found.** No
   score, band, engine label, evidence label or fragments such as “เรื่องที่
   กำลังเดินจะไปต่อได้” appear in reader copy.
9. **Where is content thinner than the Golden quality bar? — No required section
   is thin.** Candidate 0010 has 24 prediction paragraphs versus Candidate
   0009's 7, covers all three past age ranges, current age, five reader domains,
   the full rolling horizon and the corrected next period. It does not reproduce
   the Golden's unsupported early/middle/late annual buckets.
10. **Does every section contain a tangible event or change? — Yes.** Past
    sections state changed conditions, settings, duties and work agreements;
    current/domain sections state role, income, expense, relationship distance,
    recovery and support changes; the horizon states work-status, money/agreement
    and support movements; the next period states foundation and work-role
