# KnowMe BaZi Symbolic Reading V1

**Interpretation contract id:** `knowme_bazi_symbolic_reading_v1`

**Calculation dependency:** `knowme_bazi_compatibility_v1`

**Status:** implemented and validated for Owner testing on Draft PR #122; not
deployed

## Purpose

This contract adds a useful, deterministic Chinese-astrology reading to the
existing governed BaZi facts. It does not ask an AI model to improvise text.
Every reader-visible statement comes from a checked-in catalog, a fixed
element relationship, or an explicit disclosure about available input.

The reading is a symbolic self-reflection aid. It is not scientific validation,
a personality diagnosis, or a promise that a future event will occur.

## Inputs

The interpretation receives only the canonical `BaziChartModel` produced under
`knowme_bazi_compatibility_v1`:

- the Day Master stem and element;
- pillars already allowed by the Known/Unknown calculation contract;
- visible stem/branch element counts over those allowed pillars; and
- completeness and `time_known` metadata.

It does not recover suppressed values, call another API, use location to alter
the chart, or infer a birth time.

## Day Master catalog

The Day Master is the Heavenly Stem of the Day pillar. V1 maps all ten stems to
a traditional natural-image metaphor and a conservative reflection:

| Stem | Element/polarity | V1 metaphor |
|---|---|---|
| `甲 Jia` | Yang Wood | tall tree |
| `乙 Yi` | Yin Wood | vine or flowering plant |
| `丙 Bing` | Yang Fire | sun |
| `丁 Ding` | Yin Fire | candle or lamp |
| `戊 Wu` | Yang Earth | mountain or broad earth |
| `己 Ji` | Yin Earth | cultivated soil |
| `庚 Geng` | Yang Metal | raw metal or blade |
| `辛 Xin` | Yin Metal | jewel or refined metal |
| `壬 Ren` | Yang Water | great river or ocean |
| `癸 Gui` | Yin Water | rain, mist, or dew |

Each catalog entry contains exactly four reader functions: symbolic tendency,
constructive expression, a balance to watch, and one practical reflection.
These are authored paraphrases, not copied predictions. The report explicitly
says that the Day Master is a reference lens rather than a complete personality
verdict.

## Visible relationship families

For charts with at least the Year, Month, and Day pillars, V1 groups visible
element slots into five relationships relative to the Day Master element:

| Family | Fixed relationship | Reader-facing scope |
|---|---|---|
| `印 · Resource` | element that generates the Day Master | learning, information, replenishment, receiving support |
| `比劫 · Companion` | same element as the Day Master | self-direction, personal drive, peers, shared space |
| `食傷 · Output` | element generated by the Day Master | ideas, communication, making, outward expression |
| `財 · Wealth` | element controlled by the Day Master | resource management, execution, maintenance responsibility |
| `官殺 · Authority` | element that controls the Day Master | rules, boundaries, pressure, standards, responsibility |

The displayed count is the existing surface count, not a Ten-Gods strength
score. Ties are preserved. A largest count is described only as the most
visible family in the confirmed slots; it is never labelled good, bad, strong,
weak, favourable, or predictive. A zero count is explicitly not treated as an
absent life function because hidden stems, seasonality, rooting, and strength
weighting are outside V1.

## Reader-facing natal areas

Complete Known and ordinary Unknown charts project the same governed facts into
five everyday reading areas:

| Area | Deterministic basis | Allowed claim |
|---|---|---|
| Usable strengths | Day Master constructive expression + every joint-highest visible family | working modes the reader can try using |
| Work and roles | every joint-highest visible family | role/environment questions, never a fixed occupation |
| Money and resources | joint-highest families + explicit visible Wealth count | budgeting/execution reflection, never gain/loss or wealth fate |
| Relationships and shared space | every joint-highest visible family | interaction themes, never a partner or relationship outcome |
| Watch-outs and development | Day Master balance/practice + every joint-highest visible family | overuse pattern and one practical experiment |

Every area prints the exact Day Master, relationship-family label and count that
selected its copy. A tie emits all tied mappings in stable order. The finance
area additionally prints the Wealth-family count; zero produces an explicit
“no good/bad conclusion” message. The engine does not use randomness, current
time, API text generation or an AI model.

“Natal reading” here means a static reflection over the birth-chart facts. V1
does not have the hidden-stem, seasonal-strength, luck-pillar or annual-timing
inputs needed to claim when an event will occur. Boundary-partial Unknown
therefore omits all five areas rather than treating one or two pillars as a
whole chart.

## Known/Unknown fail-closed matrix

| Input case | Day Master reading | Relationship reading | Hour contribution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Known time | shown | four-pillar visible counts | included |
| Ordinary Unknown time | shown | Year/Month/Day visible counts | omitted |
| Unknown on Li Chun boundary | shown | omitted because too few invariant pillars | omitted |
| Unknown on Jie boundary | shown | omitted because too few invariant pillars | omitted |

The boundary cases keep calculated invariant facts in the technical sections,
but the interpretation stops at the Day Master instead of treating a partial
surface count as a whole-chart emphasis.

## Explicit exclusions

V1 does not calculate or claim:

- hidden stems, rooting, seasonal strength, Day Master strength, Useful God,
  combinations, clashes, transformations, or full polarity-specific Ten Gods;
- Da Yun, annual/monthly timing, favourable dates, or event forecasts;
- guaranteed career, relationship, health, wealth, finance, or legal outcomes;
- compatibility between two people; or
- AI-generated interpretations at request time.

Any of these requires a separately sourced, versioned contract and new
Known/Unknown rules. They must not be inferred from this V1 catalog.

## Projection and regression rules

Signed-in Web, the no-write Owner fixture route, plain-text export, and PDF use
the same `BaziCompatibilityReport` object. The interpretation id is printed in
the reproducibility section. Tests pin all ten Day Master entries, the five
relationship mappings, four Owner fixture behaviors, forbidden promises, and
Web/PDF use of the canonical report. Thai astrology and `product-acceptance/`
are outside this contract and must have zero diff.

Final evidence on Flutter 3.41.1 / Dart 3.11.0 is focused Flutter 79/79, full
Flutter 3,070/3,070, analyzer scoped findings 0, and 15/15 visually inspected
PDF pages. Exact artifact hashes and environment controls are recorded in
`CHINESE_ASTROLOGY_VALIDATION_V1.md`.
