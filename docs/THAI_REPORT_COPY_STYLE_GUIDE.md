# Thai Report Copy Style Guide

## Reader-facing rules

- use natural, polite Thai with one clear point per sentence
- name a tendency separately from advice; do not turn one into the other
- describe evidence in ordinary language and keep engine terms out of consumer paragraphs
- keep subjects, actions and affected life domain explicit
- avoid stacked abstract nouns and translated technical sentence structures
- preserve uncertainty; do not make deterministic health, finance or relationship claims
- Unknown-time output must omit unsupported Lagna/house facts and remain fail-closed
- every reader-visible candidate change needs before/after text, exact diff, source template, semantic assessment and Owner decision

## Anti-patterns

- demeaning transition phrases such as “แปลเป็นภาษาคน”
- internal labels such as “จุดกระตุ้น”, “ธาตุขัดกัน” or “หลักฐาน” when the reader only needs the meaning
- compressed metaphors whose subject/action is unclear
- advice presented as a prediction, or prediction presented as advice
- shortening infographic copy by truncating meaning or hiding overflow
- adding biographical events, months or certainty not supported by engine evidence

Accepted V1.5 copy is immutable. Apply these rules in a separately reviewable candidate and merge only after explicit Owner approval of the complete ledger.
