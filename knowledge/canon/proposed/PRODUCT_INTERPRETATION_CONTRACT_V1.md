# Product Interpretation Contract V1

Status: **OWNER-AUTHORIZED CONTENT DESIGN CONTRACT — NOT RUNTIME**

## Purpose

A reader prediction is authorized by a complete functional rule chain, not by requiring a source-direct event sentence for every paragraph. The chain may combine Mahabhut, Production Canon, typed forecast material already calculated by the application, and traceable research. Components need not have different authors, but one component cannot perform every role.

## Required chain

Each prediction paragraph must record the components required by its claim type:

1. **Selector** — selects the applicable Mahabhut context and age period.
2. **Domain** — establishes whether the meaning concerns work, finance, relationship, health, support, or another named life domain.
3. **Direction** — supplies strong/active/quiet, improvement/reduction, constraint, or change direction.
4. **Timing** — bounds the claim to a life period, current state, rolling 12 months, or the next life period.
5. **Conflict handling** — records `NONE`, or narrows/omits a claim when signals disagree.
6. **Certainty ceiling** — limits reader wording to what the complete chain supports.

Mahabhut is the selector and life-period backbone. Production Canon owns domain semantics. A typed `ForecastMaterialFingerprint` may supply Direction and Timing only when both the Mahabhut selector and Canon domain authority are present. Typed forecast material alone is not a prediction. Traceable research is secondary and cannot override the other components.

## Prohibitions

- no self-reference or circular derivation;
- no placement-only prediction;
- no component performing selector, domain, direction, and timing by itself;
- no date, month, amount, diagnosis, job title, event count, or causal result absent from the chain;
- no prediction converted into advice, personality copy, or methodology;
- no claim stronger than the narrowest component or unresolved conflict.

## Certainty

Reader copy may be direct within the authorized chain. It must not claim predictive accuracy. The report retains a belief-based disclaimer and requires real-world facts before important decisions.

## Unknown time

Unknown-time output remains fail-closed. A chain that requires Thai astrological day, ascendant, houses, or another time-dependent selector is omitted; the system must not substitute noon or borrow Known copy.
